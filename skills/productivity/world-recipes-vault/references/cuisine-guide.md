# Quick Cuisine Guide — Healthy Picks Per Region

Fast reference for choosing healthy dishes by cuisine type.

| Cuisine | ✅ Go-To Healthy Dish | Key Spices | Avoid |
|---------|---------------------|------------|-------|
| 🇮🇹 Italian | Grilled vegetable parmigiana | Basil, oregano, olive oil | Cream, excess cheese |
| 🇯🇵 Japanese | Salmon teriyaki, miso soup | Ginger, soy (low sodium) | Tempura, deep-fry |
| 🇲🇦 Moroccan | Vegetable tagine, fattoush | Cumin, turmeric, cinnamon | Heavy lamb, fried pastries |
| 🇲🇽 Mexican | Black bean tacos, chicken fajitas | Cumin, chili, lime | Excess cheese, sour cream |
| 🇮🇳 Indian | Palak dal, tandoori chicken | Turmeric, cumin, garam masala | Cream, ghee-heavy dishes |
| 🇹🇭 Thai | Tom yum (no seafood if allergic) | Lemongrass, ginger, lime | Heavy coconut milk, deep-fry |
| 🇬🇷 Greek | Greek salad, grilled halloumi | Oregano, lemon, olive oil | Heavy moussaka |
| 🇪🇬 Egyptian | Molokhia (low oil), okra | Garlic, coriander | Excess oil, fried bread |
| 🇰🇷 Korean | Kimchi stew, bibimbap | Gochugaru, sesame, soy | Heavy BBQ, fried chicken |

## Healthy Cooking Methods by Cuisine

| Cuisine | Preferred Method | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Italian | Grill, bake, steam | Avoid cream sauces |
| Japanese | Steam, grill, simmer (nimono) | Minimal oil |
| Moroccan | Slow-cook in tagine | Natural moisture, less oil |
| Indian | Tandoor, sauté with minimal oil | Avoid deep-fry (pakora) |
| Thai | Stir-fry (high heat, quick), steam | Request "mai nam" (no oil) |
| Greek | Grill, raw (salad) | Olive oil is healthy fat |
| Egyptian | Bake, steam, boil | Reduce oil in sautéing |
