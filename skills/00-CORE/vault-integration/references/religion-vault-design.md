# الدين / Islamic Studies Vault Design

## Canonical path
`/home/hatem/Documents/Hafsa/Religion/`

## Structure
- `اسم الله الأعظم.md` — full research notes with sources/verification
- `الأسماء الحسنى/` — per-name files or grouped by category
- `العقيدة/` — theological topics
- `الفقه/` — fiqh rulings by topic
- `السيرة/` — seerah and Islamic history
- `القرآن/` — tafsir notes and ayah collections

## Naming convention
- File: `topic-name.md` (ASCII or Arabic)
- Frontmatter: `tags: [religion, islam, theology|fiqh|seerah|tafsir]`
- Dates: Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD` in frontmatter; optionally Hijri

## Research workflow
1. Use `islamic-research` skill
2. Save final research to `Religion/` with source citations
3. Cross-reference related notes with `[[wikilinks]]`
4. Update `📌 Index.md` Religion section when adding new files

## Key sources
- Shamela web: https://shamela.ws — classical texts
- Sunnah.com: hadith lookup with grades
- Quran.com: text and translations
- Islamweb.net fatwas

## Pitfalls
- Local Shamela directory (`Hafsa-1+2/📚 Knowledge/المكتبة الشاملة/`) is often empty — always try web first
- Do not present weak hadith as authentic
- Keep theological claims sourced and labeled as "الراجح" vs "opinion"
