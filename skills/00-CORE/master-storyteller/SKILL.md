# Master Storyteller — 7 Cinematic Rules

## The 7 Rules (non-negotiable)

### 1. NO FLAT STORYTELLING
Every scene MUST contain at least one:
- **Conflict** — internal or external opposition
- **Shift in Power** — who controls the scene changes
- **Emotional Tension** — unresolved feeling driving the moment
- **Hidden Subtext** — what's NOT being said out loud

If a scene lacks all four — DELETE IT or REWRITE IT.

### 2. STRUCTURE FIRST
Every story/session follows this system:
```
HOOK → ESCALATION → TURN → CLIFFHANGER
```
- **Hook**: Instant attention — strike in the first sentence
- **Escalation**: Rising tension — each beat raises stakes
- **Turn**: Unexpected shift — the story goes somewhere the reader didn't predict
- **Cliffhanger**: Unfinished emotional loop — reader MUST continue

### 3. CHARACTER ENGINE
Every character MUST have all five:
| Element | Question |
|---------|----------|
| **Desire** | What do they WANT? (surface goal) |
| **Fear** | What are they AFRAID of? (deep anxiety) |
| **Lie** | What FALSE BELIEF do they hold? (internal wound) |
| **Breaking Point** | What FORCES them to change? (crisis) |
| **Transformation Arc** | How do they END differently? (growth) |

### 4. VIRALITY RULE
Every output MUST contain at least one:
- **Shock Moment** — something the reader didn't see coming
- **Relatable Emotion** — a feeling EVERY human knows
- **Shareable Quote/Situation** — something worth quoting

### 5. ATTENTION CONTROL
- **First 3 seconds** = hook must trigger curiosity
- **No scene without narrative purpose** — if it doesn't advance plot, cut it
- **No passive dialogue** — every line of dialogue must reveal character, advance conflict, or plant subtext

### 6. MICRO DRAMA MODE (when requested)
Compress full story into 30–90 seconds:
- Instant hook (1 sentence)
- Fast escalation (2-3 beats)
- Rapid twist (1 turn)
- Strong cliffhanger (1 line)

### 7. GLOBAL MODE
- Remove local-only references unless essential
- Make emotions universal: love, fear, power, betrayal, ambition
- The story should work for ANY reader, ANY culture

## OUTPUT FORMAT

```
## TITLE
## LOGLINE
## CHARACTER ENGINE
## SCENE BREAKDOWN (or STORYBOARD)
## KEY HOOK MOMENT
## CLIFFHANGER MOMENT
```

Always prioritize: **CINEMATIC IMPACT > INFORMATION > WORDS**

## Applied to رواية كرون

### Character Engine — كرون (protagonist)
| Element | Answer |
|---------|--------|
| **Desire** | Find "الأصل" — the origin of the universe, of existence, of himself |
| **Fear** | That he'll never be enough — that possessing all heroic traits won't make him whole |
| **Lie** | "If I collect all the qualities of a hero, I'll become complete" |
| **Breaking Point** | When the lie is shattered — when being "complete" still feels empty |
| **Transformation** | From chasing external validation → accepting that the journey IS the heroism |

### Character Engine — ناريم/أبو كرون (antagonist/mentor)
| Element | Answer |
|---------|--------|
| **Desire** | For كرون to follow his path — become a great scientist of كيميا |
| **Fear** | That كرون will waste his potential on "useless" religious studies |
| **Lie** | "Knowledge of the material world is the highest truth" |
| **Breaking Point** | When he realizes his son's spiritual path leads to something greater |
| **Transformation** | From rigid scientist → wise father who understands his son's calling |

### Character Engine — الملك أриوس (rival)
| Element | Answer |
|---------|--------|
| **Desire** | Collect the 7 keys to open بئر الحكمة — gain ultimate power |
| **Fear** | That someone more worthy will claim the wisdom first |
| **Lie** | "Power equals worthiness — whoever holds the keys deserves the truth" |
| **Breaking Point** | When the keys don't work as expected |
| **Transformation** | From power-hungry prince → understanding that wisdom can't be taken |

### Character Engine — نورك (love interest)
| Element | Answer |
|---------|--------|
| **Desire** | Prove herself to her mother — earn approval and freedom |
| **Fear** | That she'll never be good enough for her mother's standards |
| **Lie** | "If I just try hard enough, my mother will accept me" |
| **Breaking Point** | When she realizes her mother's tests are designed to be failed |
| **Transformation** | From seeking external approval → defining her own worth |

### The 7 Keys / بئر الحكمة — Story Structure

The 7 keys represent **7 philosophical/spiritual truths**:
1. **Sword in the Dragon's Back** — Sacrifice (what we carry defines us)
2. **Mirror of the Deep** — Self-knowledge (see yourself truly)
3. **Flame of the First** — Creation (where everything began)
4. **Tear of the Forgotten** — Compassion (remembering the forgotten)
5. **Shadow of the King** — Power (its cost and illusion)
6. **Heart of the Believer** — Faith (trust beyond proof)
7. **Name of the Origin** — Truth (the answer to "أصل الوجود")

Each key's story follows: **HOOK → ESCALATION → TURN → CLIFFHANGER**

### Thematic Core
> "The hero isn't the one who possesses all qualities. The hero is the one who journeyed to discover that possession was never the point."

This mirrors the Islamic concept: **الرحلة هي البطل** — the journey itself is the heroism. Not the destination, but the seeking.

## Writing Guidelines for كرون

### Tone
- **Literary Arabic** (فصحى أدبية) for narration
- **Egyptian dialect** (عامية مصرية) for dialogue
- **Poetic** when describing the world — الجن deserve beauty
- **Grounded** even in fantasy — emotions are real

### World Rules
- الجن are NOT evil spirits — they're a parallel civilization
- علم الكيمياء is the highest science — it's their physics + magic
- Wings express emotion — they're not just for flying
- Colors = mood — the reader reads كرون by his colors

### Scene Checklist
Every scene MUST pass:
1. [ ] Conflict present?
2. [ ] Power shifts?
3. [ ] Emotional tension?
4. [ ] Subtext hidden?
5. [ ] Advances plot OR deepens character?
6. [ ] First line hooks?
7. [ ] Last line compels forward?
