# كرون Scene Breakdown — 7 Rules Applied

Session: 2026-06-22. First 3 scenes storyboarded for the كرون novel.

## Scene 1: القناع الذهبي (The Golden Mask)
- **Hook**: "القناع الذهبي كان يسخر مني — ليس لأنه ثقيل، بل لأنه لم يكن وجهي."
- **Conflict**: كرون vs his social mask (golden mask = expectations of his status)
- **Power Shift**: Mask controls him → he removes it → mask speaks (it has agency)
- **Emotional Tension**: Identity crisis — who is he without the mask?
- **Subtext**: The mask isn't protection — it's a prison
- **Shock**: The mask TALKS — it's controlling him, not the other way around
- **Cliffhanger**: "أطلقت القناع في الهاوية — وانتظرت السقوط."
- **File**: `رواية-كرون/05-المشاهد-المكتوبة/001-القناع-الذهبي.md`

## Scene 2: سؤال الأب (The Father's Question)
- **Hook**: "أبي لم يسألني كيف حالي — سألني: 'هل بدأت في دراسة الكيميا بعد؟'"
- **Conflict**: Father wants him to be a scientist → كرون wants to find الأصل
- **Power Shift**: Father starts in control → كرون refuses → father uses emotion → كرون withdraws
- **Emotional Tension**: Love + conflict — father loves son but doesn't understand him
- **Subtext**: Every question from the father is an invitation to return to the "right" path
- **Shock**: كرون leaves without saying goodbye — talks about the origin of the sky while fleeing
- **Cliffhanger**: "هل كسبت نفسي؟"
- **File**: `رواية-كرون/05-المشاهد-المكتوبة/002-سؤال-الأب.md`

## Scene 3: البنت اللي خافت (The Girl Who Was Afraid)
- **Hook**: "نظرت إليّ كأنني الخطر — وأنا لم أفعل شيئاً سوى الوجود."
- **Conflict**: نورك avoids كرون — he doesn't understand why, she doesn't understand herself
- **Power Shift**: كرون shows interest → نورك controls through rejection → كرون discovers her rejection is protection
- **Emotional Tension**: First meeting — fear disguised as rejection — curiosity disguised as indifference
- **Subtext**: نورك doesn't hate كرون — she's afraid of what might happen if she gets close
- **Shock**: نورك LEFT the book deliberately — she brought كرون to her!
- **Cliffhanger**: "كانت تبحث عني قبل أن أبحث عنها."
- **File**: `رواية-كرون/05-المشاهد-المكتوبة/003-البنت-اللي-خافت.md`

## Key Techniques Used
- **Color as narrative tool**: كرون's aura changes color with emotion (green→blue = peace→sadness)
- **Wings as emotional barometer**: Wings appear when he's fleeing, not when he's flying
- **Objects as bridges**: The book between كرون and نورك — knowledge connects before emotions
- **Silence as dialogue**: What's NOT said matters more than what is
