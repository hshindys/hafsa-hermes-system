# Medical Research Pipeline

Systematic medical research workflow: literature search → screening → synthesis → report.

## Workflow

### 1. Determine the query type
- Drug/medication research
- Disease/condition overview
- Clinical trial search
- Diagnostic criteria
- Treatment guidelines (WHO, NICE, ADA, AHA/ACC)
- Arabic medical library resources

### 2. Search sources
Primary: PubMed, Google Scholar, WHO, FDA/EMA. Fallback: Cochrane, UpToDate, ScienceDirect.

### 3. Read and screen
- Extract: title, authors, year, key findings, evidence level (I–V)
- Keep RCTs and systematic reviews
- Filter editorials and low-level case reports

### 4. Synthesize
- Background
- Top 3–5 cited studies
- Relevant treatment guidelines
- Drug interactions when applicable
- Actionable clinical note

### 5. Safety disclaimer
Always include: "⚠️ This is informational only."
