# OODA Loop / Infinite Brain Concept (Summary)

Source: YouTube video — OODA Loop + Infinite Brain system for AI agents

## The 4 OODA Stages (what AI is good at)
- **Observe** — AI can monitor 10,000 inputs/hour (vs 100 for humans)
- **Decide** — Fast decisions with well-defined rules
- **Act** — Computer use, workflow execution, presentations

## The Missing Stage: Orient (what AI lacks)
AI doesn't know:
- What matters to you personally/your company
- Your philosophy, values, constraints
- Details of your work and audience
- The conceptual framework that makes decisions correct

## The Solution: Infinite Brain
A structured knowledge system that provides the Orient layer:
- **Ingestion** — Capture everything (email, Slack, YouTube, meetings, blogs)
- **Knowledge Graph** — Structured storage (like Obsidian)
- **Routing** — Deterministic filters for spam, AI for complex categorization
- **Dispositions** — Auto-generated next steps from observations
- **Wagers** — AI predicts impact of changes before executing
- **Wager Verdicts** — Review: did the change actually improve things?

## Application to Vaults
The OODA structure (00-Inbox → 01-Projects → 02-Knowledge → 03-Archive → 04-System) implements the Orient layer for AI agents, enabling them to:
1. Ingest new inputs into 00-Inbox
2. Orient against 02-Knowledge
3. Decide on actions in 01-Projects
4. Act (update files, send messages)
5. Archive completed work to 03-Archive
